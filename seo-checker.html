What We Check

On-Page & Technical SEO Basics

This tool checks the foundational on-page and technical SEO elements that affect how search engines understand and rank your site — title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, mobile-friendliness, and the presence of robots.txt and sitemap.xml. It's not a replacement for full SEO platforms like Ahrefs or SEMrush (which track backlinks, keyword rankings and competitor data), but it's a fast, free way to catch the basics that are often missed.

FAQ

Common Questions

Why didn't the automatic check work?

Some sites block requests from outside your browser (CORS restrictions) or sit behind protections like Cloudflare. If automatic fetching fails, use the "paste HTML source" option instead — it works for any page.

Does this check backlinks or keyword rankings?

No. This tool focuses on on-page and technical basics only. Backlink and ranking data requires paid third-party APIs (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz) which this free tool doesn't use.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The page content is analyzed entirely in your browser and never stored or logged by this site.

What's a good score?

80+ indicates solid on-page fundamentals. Scores below 60 usually mean missing basics (title/meta tags, alt text, robots.txt) that are quick wins to fix.
