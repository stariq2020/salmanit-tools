Outlook Won't Open, Hangs or Closes Straight Away

When classic Outlook will not open, the usual suspects are a faulty add-in, a stuck background process, a damaged profile or a damaged Office installation. The right order is to start Outlook in Safe Mode first, because it takes seconds and tells you whether an add-in is responsible. Only then move on to repairs. Your email stays on the server throughout.

This guide provides general troubleshooting guidance. It cannot directly inspect or repair your computer. Takes about 20–45 min; difficulty: moderate; risk: low. Last reviewed 2026-10-13.

What each symptom suggests

  • Closes immediatelyOften an add-in that crashes on start. Safe Mode (outlook.exe /safe) will normally reveal it.
  • Stuck on "Loading profile"A large data file, a stuck process or a damaged profile. Ending OUTLOOK.EXE in Task Manager and restarting helps first.
  • "Cannot start Microsoft Outlook"A damaged profile or Office files. Repair Office and, if needed, create a new profile.
  • New Outlook for WindowsDifferent app: reset it from Settings > Apps, and check you can read mail at outlook.office.com.

Common signs

  • Outlook closes immediately
  • Stuck on "Loading profile" or the splash screen
  • "Cannot start Microsoft Outlook"
  • "Outlook is not responding"

The order we try fixes in

The guided troubleshooter personalises this order from your answers. Here is the general sequence, safest and quickest first:

  1. Close any hidden Outlook process and restartOutlook can keep running invisibly after a crash and block a fresh start. (5 min)
  2. Open Outlook in Safe ModeSafe Mode starts Outlook without add-ins or customisations. (3 min)
  3. Disable add-ins one by oneAdd-ins (for example for meeting tools, CRM or PDF) are the most common cause of Outlook crashes. (15 min)
  4. Repair Microsoft 365 appsThis checks Office's files and replaces any that are damaged. (20–40 min)
  5. Create a new Outlook profileA profile stores your account settings. (20 min)
  6. Check your mail on the webSigning in to Outlook on the web shows whether your account itself works. (5 min)

Error codes linked to this problem

  • 0x80070005 – Access denied (E_ACCESSDENIED)Windows or an app was refused permission to read or change something. Common causes include missing administrator rights, security software blocking the action, or damaged permissions.
  • 0x80004005 – Unspecified error (E_FAIL)A catch-all failure code with no specific cause. Treat it as a symptom: look at what you were doing when it appeared.

Quick answers

Will I lose emails if I make a new profile?

Mail stored on your Microsoft 365 or Exchange mailbox stays on the server and re-downloads. Take care with local PST files, which exist only on this PC: do not delete them.

What is Safe Mode in Outlook?

It starts Outlook without add-ins and custom settings. It changes nothing permanently, and closing and reopening Outlook normally returns to normal.

Which Outlook am I using?

Classic Outlook has a ribbon with File, Home and Send/Receive. The new Outlook for Windows has a simpler look and a New Outlook toggle in some versions.

Can I still read my mail meanwhile?

Yes: sign in at outlook.office.com in a browser. That also confirms whether your account itself is fine.

If it is a work or school device

Some of these steps may be blocked by your organisation's policies. If a step is unavailable or asks for an administrator password, stop and contact your IT team. Never share passwords or BitLocker recovery keys.
