AI summaries can miss details or get things wrong. Check anything important against the original PDF.

🔒 What stays private

The PDF file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Only the extracted text goes to the AI, and only after you tick the box and click Summarize.

📚 Long documents

Big PDFs are split into parts, summarized part by part, then combined. Pick a page range to focus on one section and keep it quick.

🖼️ Scanned PDFs

Scans are pictures of text, so there's nothing to read. This tool doesn't do OCR — it will tell you if it finds no text.
