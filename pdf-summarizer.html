Summary

This tool picks the most important sentences from your PDF — it doesn't rewrite or interpret them. Check anything important against the original.

🔒 Fully private

Everything happens in your browser. No upload, no account, no AI service — the text never leaves your device.

🧮 How it works

It finds the document's key terms, scores every sentence by how many it contains, then keeps the best non-repetitive ones in their original order.

⚠️ Limits

Works best on English prose such as reports and articles. Scanned PDFs have no readable text (no OCR), and tables or slides summarize poorly.

Need to split out pages first, or check whether a PDF has real text? Use the PDF Toolkit.
