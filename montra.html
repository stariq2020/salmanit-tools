Install Guide

How to Install on Android

Android warns about any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. It isn't a virus warning. It's a standard prompt, and the exact wording and menus vary a little between phone brands.

01

Download & open

Tap Download for Android above, then open Montra.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder. Chrome may say this type of file can harm your device. Choose to keep it.

02

Allow this source

If it says Install blocked, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.

03

Play Protect

If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

04

Install & open

Tap Install, then Open. You can switch the "allow this source" setting off again afterwards.

To check the file is the one published here, compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one at the top of this page. Only install Montra from this page or from someone you trust.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Montra?

Montra is a private expense tracker and budget app for Android. You add what you spend (by typing or by voice), and it shows your remaining balance, recurring bills, budgets, savings goals and spending insights. Your data stays on your phone.

Why does Android show a warning when I install it?

Android warns about every app that doesn't come from the Google Play Store. It is a standard security prompt for any app installed from a downloaded file, not a sign that something is wrong. Follow the install steps above: allow installs from your browser or Files app, then choose Install anyway if Play Protect asks.

Is Montra safe?

Montra has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it runs no servers, so it never receives your data. The database on your phone is encrypted, and you can lock the app with a PIN and optional fingerprint or face unlock. You can compare the SHA-256 checksum on this page with the file you downloaded.

Does Montra upload my data?

No. Your expenses, bills and settings are stored only on your phone. The only time anything leaves it is if you choose to make a backup to your own Google Drive, and that backup is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase you choose before it is uploaded.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Montra works fully offline. The internet is only used if you choose to back up to or restore from Google Drive.

What happens if I forget my PIN or backup passphrase?

Neither can be recovered, because they are never stored anywhere else. A lost PIN means clearing the app's data, which erases everything on that phone, so make an encrypted backup first. A backup without its passphrase cannot be opened by anyone.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 or newer.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Montra is Android only.
