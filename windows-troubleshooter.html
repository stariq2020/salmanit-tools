Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool scan or repair my computer?

No. A website cannot see inside your PC. This tool asks questions about what you are seeing and then gives you reviewed, step-by-step instructions that you carry out yourself. It never claims to have scanned or fixed anything.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It is completely free. There is no sign-up, no login and nothing to install.

Is my description sent anywhere?

No. By default everything you type is processed in your browser and goes nowhere. An optional AI helper only appears if the site owner has switched it on, and it only runs if you tick a clear consent box first. Fixes always come from the reviewed guides, never from AI.

Does it cover Windows 10 and Windows 11?

Yes. Instructions adapt to the version you choose. Windows 10 reached its standard end of support on 14 October 2025, although some devices have Extended Security Updates or other support arrangements. If you are unsure which version you have, press the Windows key + R, type winver and press Enter.

How safe are the steps?

Safe, reversible steps always come first. The tool will not tell you to edit the registry, disable your antivirus or firewall, format drives, reset Windows or change BIOS settings. Anything risky is flagged with a warning or passed to a technician. No fix is ever guaranteed.

What if my PC belongs to my employer or school?

Some steps may be blocked by IT policy. If a step is greyed out or asks for an administrator password, stop and contact your IT team. The summary you can copy, print or save as a PDF is designed to be handed to them.

Can I share the results with IT support?

Yes. At the end you can copy the summary, print it or download it as a PDF. It lists the problem, your answers, likely causes (labelled as hypotheses), the steps you tried and what happened. It never includes passwords or recovery keys.

My problem is not listed. What now?

Describe it in your own words, including any error message. If the tool cannot match it, it will say so and let you browse by category instead of guessing. You can also search the error-code library on this page.
