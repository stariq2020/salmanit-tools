Are my calculations sent anywhere?

No — everything happens in your browser.

Every calculator on this page runs entirely as JavaScript in your browser. Nothing you type — salary, bill amounts, dates, mortgage figures — is uploaded, stored, or sent to Salman IT Tools or anyone else.

FAQ

General Questions

Is the Life Calculator free?

Yes — every calculator on this page is completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no usage limits.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. All calculations run directly in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any server.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes — every calculator is designed mobile-first, with full-width inputs, touch-friendly controls and a numeric keyboard where relevant.

Can I bookmark or share a specific calculator?

Yes. Each calculator has its own URL (for example life-calculator.html#discount) that opens directly to that tool.
