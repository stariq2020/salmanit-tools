How To Use

Generating Your QR Code

Creating a QR code with this tool takes just a few seconds and works entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server, so your data stays private. Choose the type of content you want to encode (a website link, plain text, an email address, a phone number, or WiFi credentials), enter the details, and the QR code is generated instantly on screen.

Once generated, you can download the QR code as a PNG image to use in printed materials, digital signage, social media posts, or anywhere else you need it. Because the code is created locally on your device, you can generate as many QR codes as you like with no limits and no account required.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do QR codes expire?

No. A QR code generated here encodes your content directly (a static QR code), so it never expires and will keep working for as long as the destination (e.g. your website) exists.

Is there a limit on how many I can create?

No limit. Since everything runs in your browser, you can generate as many QR codes as you need — completely free.

Will my data be stored anywhere?

No. Nothing you enter is uploaded, logged or stored. The QR code is generated locally on your device using your browser's processing power.

What's the best format for printing?

Download the PNG and print at a size of at least 2x2cm for reliable scanning. For large prints (posters, banners), test the scan from the expected viewing distance first.
